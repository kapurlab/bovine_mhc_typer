## ⚠️ INVESTIGATE — Class I diploid-split / BoLA-6 artifact

**Symptom.** In the `20260731` BosEx SUP run, **17 of 21 confirmed classical calls are
`BoLA-6*091:01` + `*092:02`**, and in most barcodes they come from the **same cluster** as
a dominant + het-second pair (e.g. bc07 c_5, bc09 c_7, bc14 c_4, bc17 c_7, bc22 c_6 — all
`092:02` dominant / `091:01` second, "3het"). Red flags: **bc10 c_268 called `092:02` for
BOTH** dominant and second; **bc06 c_4** is pipeline-flagged `multiallelic → paralog-mix`.
The highest-read clusters per barcode are mostly **NON_CLASSICAL** (BoLA-NC2/NC9, Bogr-NC9);
the BoLA-6 classical calls come from smaller clusters.

**Hypothesis.** `091:01` and `092:02` differ by ~3 SNPs. The pipeline clusters (id 0.88) to
separate paralogs, then runs a **bcftools ploidy-2 split within each cluster** to emit "two
alleles." When a cluster is really *one* BoLA-6 allele with a few noisy/variant positions,
the ploidy-2 split **manufactures a spurious second allele**, and because `091:01`/`092:02`
are IPD neighbors the ALT consensus lands on the other at 100%. So many "2 confident alleles"
are likely **one real allele + a split artifact** — the classic "can't assume ploidy-2 inside
a collapsed multi-copy cluster" problem, and why Class I is provisional.

**To do.**
1. Inspect the per-cluster `.vcf` (AD/DP) for the 3 het positions between `091:01`/`092:02`:
   strong balanced ALT support (real het) vs low-fraction noise (artifact)?
2. **Tighten the diploid split**: require a minimum ALT fraction / read support before
   emitting a second allele; flag/suppress when allele-1 and allele-2 are the **same gene,
   adjacent in IPD** (vs genuinely different genes).
3. **Cross-amplicon confirmation**: reconcile with Bov7/11 + 5′UTR — if `091:01` doesn't
   independently appear, it's a split artifact.
4. Note the **amplicon primer skew**: BosEx over-represents BoLA-6 / non-classical; Bov7/11
   gave BoLA-1/2. Expected, but relevant to interpreting single-amplicon calls.
